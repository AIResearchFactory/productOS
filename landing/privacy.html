Security & Architecture

Local Data Privacy by Design

How ProductOS safeguards your proprietary PRDs, roadmaps, and customer research.

1. Zero Central Servers

ProductOS operates entirely on your local computer. There are no central ProductOS servers tracking your usage, storing your documents, or recording your chat histories.

2. Native OS Keychain Encryption

API keys and sensitive secrets are stored using OS-level secure storage (macOS Keychain, Windows Credential Manager, Linux Secret Service). Internal secrets are encrypted using AES-256-GCM.

3. Plain Markdown Files & Total Ownership

Your data is stored in standard Markdown files within your local user directory. No vendor lock-in, proprietary database locks, or export friction. You can back up or sync your workspace using Git, Dropbox, or any tool of choice.

4. 100% Offline Local LLM Support

Pair ProductOS with Ollama or local LLM runners for complete offline air-gapped security. Ideal for enterprises with strict compliance policies.
